Privacy policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

Who this covers

This policy covers the "Lee Thambu" Google app, an internal tool used only by Lee Kibe Thambu (Nairobi, Kenya) to run his own business. It is not offered to the public, and no one else signs in to it.

What Google data it uses

With the owner's permission, the app uses the owner's own Google account to:

  • send and read the owner's business email (Gmail)
  • read and update the owner's spreadsheets, documents and presentations (Sheets, Docs, Slides)
  • store and share files in the owner's Drive
  • manage the owner's calendar and tasks

It only acts on data in the owner's own account.

How data is stored and shared

Data stays in the owner's Google account and on the owner's own computer and private cloud workspace. It is not sold, rented or shared with third parties, and it is not used for advertising.

Use of Google user data

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account → Third-party connections.
